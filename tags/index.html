Discover the collection

One idea.
Three ways to tell it.

Choose the structure that matches how much your brand needs to say.

One-piece tag

A clear introduction.

One strong piece places your logo, name and key message in one elegant, easy-to-read format.

  • Introduces your brand immediately
  • Keeps the presentation clean
  • Works beautifully for minimal collections

Two-piece tag

Identity, with room to say more.

Separate your main brand identity from supporting product information without making the design feel crowded.

  • One piece can carry your logo
  • One piece can explain size, material or collection
  • Layering creates a richer tactile experience

Three-piece tag

A complete brand story.

A three-piece system turns a simple tag into a layered brand moment—identity, information and emotion working together.

  • Main logo or collection piece
  • Product, size or care information
  • Brand story, QR code or personal message

Questions

Before you choose your tags.

A few useful answers before starting your project.

Should I choose one-piece, two-piece or three-piece tags?

Choose one-piece for a minimal presentation, two-piece when you need brand identity and supporting information, and three-piece when you want a complete layered story.

Can Luxsome help with the design?

Yes. We can help organise the information, recommend the right structure and develop a design that fits your wider packaging system.

Can the tag match my box, tissue and ribbon?

Yes. Coordinating each piece is central to how Luxsome approaches complete packaging systems.

Can I add a QR code?

Yes. A QR code can connect customers to your website, product information, campaign or social platform.

Do you provide strings and eyelets?

Available attachment options can be discussed during your project based on the selected style and finish.
