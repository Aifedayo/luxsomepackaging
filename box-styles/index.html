CHOOSE YOUR FOUNDATION

The structure creates the experience.

The shape of a rigid box influences how it opens, how the product is presented and how memorable the unboxing feels. Start with the structure that best suits your product; Luxsome then develops the visual and tactile character around it.

01

Shoulder Box

Structured. Refined. Designed for presentation.

A two-part rigid box with an inner shoulder that creates a distinctive reveal between the lid and base. Its layered construction gives the box a particularly composed and premium appearance.

Best suited for

Fashion pieces, accessories, jewellery, gifting and elevated product presentation.

Experience

Structured · Refined · Layered reveal

  • Strong premium presentation
  • Beautiful layered opening
  • Excellent with speciality-paper finishes
  • Works well for highly curated unboxing systems
Build a packaging system with this structure →

02

Magnetic Flap Box

A premium opening experience with a satisfying close.

A rigid presentation box with a hinged flap secured by concealed magnets. It gives customers a smooth, deliberate opening experience while providing a broad exterior surface for brand expression.

Best suited for

Clothing, modest fashion, accessories, gifting and complete packaging systems.

Experience

Premium · Versatile · Satisfying closure

  • Secure concealed magnetic closure
  • Generous branding surface
  • Highly versatile across fashion categories
  • Pairs beautifully with tissue, ribbon and cards
Build a packaging system with this structure →

03

Tray-in-Bed Box

Clean construction with a beautifully contained presentation.

The product sits within a structured tray integrated into an outer rigid base. The result is an organised presentation with a clean sense of depth and a deliberate product reveal.

Best suited for

Scarves, accessories, smaller garments, jewellery and carefully arranged product sets.

Experience

Clean · Contained · Layered

  • Product sits neatly within the structure
  • Excellent for organised presentation
  • Creates a subtle layered reveal
  • Works well with premium speciality papers
Build a packaging system with this structure →

04

Door Style Box

Made for the reveal.

A rigid presentation structure with two panels that open outward like doors, placing the product at the centre of the experience. It is ideal when the opening itself should feel ceremonial and memorable.

Best suited for

Statement pieces, premium gifting, special collections and product launches.

Experience

Dramatic · Memorable · Reveal-led

  • Distinctive double-door opening
  • Creates a highly memorable reveal
  • Large surfaces for brand expression
  • Ideal for statement packaging experiences
Build a packaging system with this structure →

05

Tray-in-Tray Box

A layered structure designed around discovery.

A rigid construction where one tray works together with another to create a clean, layered presentation. It is useful when products or packaging pieces need to feel organised while retaining a premium reveal.

Best suited for

Accessories, curated sets, fashion pieces and coordinated product presentations.

Experience

Organised · Layered · Premium

  • Layered tray presentation
  • Strong rigid construction
  • Useful for coordinated sets
  • Excellent canvas for speciality-paper finishes
