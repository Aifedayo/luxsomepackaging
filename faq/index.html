Products and systems

Packaging

We create coordinated packaging products including:

  • Custom rigid boxes
  • Hang tags and product tags
  • Thank-you cards and notes
  • Branded envelopes
  • Tissue wrappers
  • Branded ribbons
  • Stickers and seals
  • Complete packaging systems

A complete packaging system is a coordinated collection of branded packaging pieces designed to work together.

Instead of receiving only a box, your customer may experience matching tags, tissue, ribbon, stickers, thank-you cards and envelopes.

This creates consistency, increases perceived value and makes the complete unboxing experience more memorable.

Available rigid box options may include:

  • Magnetic flap boxes
  • Shoulder boxes
  • Tray-in-bed boxes
  • Door-style boxes
  • Gate boxes
  • Collapsible magnetic boxes

Availability depends on your preferred dimensions, quantity, material and production requirements.

Pricing and confirmation

Payment

Unless otherwise agreed:

  • A 70% upfront payment is required after the final design has been approved and before production begins.
  • The remaining 30% balance must be paid before collection, dispatch or delivery.

Some projects may also require a separate design or sampling fee before production.

Website prices may represent launch prices, package prices or starting prices.

Your final quotation can be affected by:

  • Quantity
  • Box size and structure
  • Material selection
  • Printing and finishing
  • Number of packaging pieces
  • Design complexity
  • Delivery destination

The quotation you approve will show what is included in your specific order.

Please contact us immediately if you need to cancel an order.

Where design work, material sourcing or production has already begun, reasonable costs already incurred may be deducted from any refundable amount.

Custom products that have already been produced to your approved specifications may not be refundable unless they are defective or applicable law provides otherwise.

Please read our Refund & Returns Policy for additional information.
