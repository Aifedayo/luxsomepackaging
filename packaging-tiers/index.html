One brand. Different stages.

Your packaging should grow with your business.

A new brand does not always need the same packaging system as an established label. Our tiers help you invest in the details that matter now, while leaving room for the experience to grow later.

01

Tier 1

Essential

A focused first step beyond generic packaging.

Who is it for?

Essential is designed for new and growing fashion or lifestyle brands that want to look more intentional without committing to a large packaging system immediately.

How it helps your brand

  • Replaces generic packaging with a more professional presentation.
  • Introduces a clear and consistent brand identity.
  • Focuses your budget on the most important packaging touchpoints.
  • Creates a foundation that can expand as orders and customer expectations increase.
Best for

New brands, smaller product ranges, first packaging upgrades and brands working with a focused budget.

Tier 2

Signature

A coordinated experience for a growing brand.

Who is it for?

Signature is for growing brands that already have a clear identity but need their packaging pieces to feel more connected, recognisable and established.

How it helps your brand

  • Connects multiple packaging pieces through one visual identity.
  • Makes the unboxing experience feel more thoughtful and complete.
  • Gives customers more opportunities to remember your name and presentation.
  • Helps a growing business look more established without building the largest system.
Best for

Scaling brands, regular product launches, growing online stores and businesses ready for a recognisable unboxing experience.

03

Tier 3

Prestige

A complete premium system built around the customer experience.

Who is it for?

Prestige is designed for established fashion and lifestyle brands whose products, pricing and customer expectations require a fuller, more layered presentation.

How it helps your brand

  • Creates a considered experience from the outer box to the smallest finishing detail.
  • Builds anticipation as each packaging layer is opened.
  • Gives your brand more space to communicate its story, care and value.
  • Supports premium positioning by making the presentation feel equal to the product.
Best for

Established labels, luxury collections, high-value products, gifting, VIP orders, launches and press packages.

Your own combination

Bespoke

Choose the exact pieces your brand needs.

Who is it for?

Bespoke is for brands whose requirements do not fit neatly into a preset tier, or brands that already have some packaging pieces and only need selected additions.

How it helps your brand

  • Gives you control over the pieces included in your packaging system.
  • Prevents you from paying for items you already have or do not need.
  • Supports unusual products, launches and campaign-specific requirements.
  • Allows the packaging system to be developed around your exact priorities.
Best for

Unique requirements, special launches, gifting campaigns, uncommon product sizes and brands with existing packaging.

Common questions

Choosing with confidence.

Can I change the pieces in a preset tier?

Preset tiers are structured around a specific packaging experience. When you need a different combination, Bespoke is the more suitable route.

Can I begin with Essential and upgrade later?

Yes. The tiers are designed to give your brand room to grow. You can begin with the key pieces and expand the system as your needs change.

Which tier is the most popular?

Signature is a popular choice because it offers a balanced combination of branded touchpoints without requiring the fullest system.

Is Prestige only for very large brands?

No. Prestige is also suitable for premium launches, gifting campaigns, limited collections and products that need a more complete presentation.

What should I choose when I am still unsure?

Use the packaging builder or start a project. Luxsome can help identify the system that best matches your product, budget and customer experience.
