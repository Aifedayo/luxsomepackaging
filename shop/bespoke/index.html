Why these prices are structured this way

Public Lagos pricing commonly starts at higher quantities such as 50 or 100 pieces. Luxsome's 25-system minimum carries a higher per-unit production cost while keeping premium packaging accessible to growing brands.

Standard logo finishes

Standard print, matte vinyl and Metallic finish do not attract an additional finishing fee where suitable for the selected material and design.

Published tier prices now cover standard finished-box size bands from up to 1 kg through 7 kg volumetric weight at the stated 25-system minimum. Speciality papers outside our standard collection, premium finishes and complex structures may change the final quotation.

These figures are market-informed guide prices for Lagos as of August 2026. Material availability and supplier costs can change, so every order is confirmed after dimensions, artwork and production details are reviewed.
