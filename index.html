Frequently Asked Questions

Ordering custom packaging from Luxsome

Clear answers for brands searching for a packaging company or box manufacturer in Lagos.

View all questions →
Does Luxsome manufacture custom packaging boxes in Lagos?

Yes. We design and produce custom packaging boxes in Lagos and serve fashion, beauty and lifestyle brands across Nigeria.

What types of boxes can you produce?

Our rigid box options include magnetic flap boxes, shoulder boxes, tray-in-bed boxes, collapsible boxes and other custom structures developed around your product.

What makes Luxsome rigid boxes different?

Our rigid boxes are wrapped in carefully selected premium speciality papers. We consider the paper's colour, texture and compatibility with the intended branding finish so the material becomes part of the brand experience, not just a covering.

Can you create a complete branding and packaging system?

Yes. We can coordinate your box with hang tags, cards, envelopes, tissue paper, sticker seals and ribbon so every piece follows one visual direction.

Do you work only with businesses in Lagos?

No. Production is managed in Lagos, and we work with brands and deliver packaging projects across Nigeria.

How do I get pricing for my packaging?

Browse our pricing page for available packaging systems or use the Packaging Builder to receive a recommendation based on the pieces your brand needs.
